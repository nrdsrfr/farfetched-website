# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Three audiences, in rough priority order:
- **Executives hiring an advisor:** CEO/CTO/COO-level buyers at iGaming and other regulated or high-growth digital businesses, deciding whether to book an intro call.
- **Founders and visionaries:** people with an early or "far-fetched" idea, deciding whether Far Fetched Labs can turn it into a prototype.
- **Peers and recruiters:** industry contacts checking Jesse Cary's credibility, often arriving from LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose
Far-Fetched Advisory and Consulting (farfetched.ltd) is the marketing site for Jesse Cary's consultancy. It exists to turn credible visitors into a 30-minute intro call or an email. Success is a booked call or inbound enquiry.

## Positioning
Operator turned advisor: 20+ years as a hands-on engineering leader (former VP of Engineering) in regulated iGaming and adjacent sectors. The advice comes from having run platforms and teams at scale, not from generic consulting.

## Operating Context
Static Jekyll site (minima-based theme override) served via GitHub Pages on the custom domain www.farfetched.ltd. Source lives in `docs/`; local run is `bundle exec jekyll serve --source docs`. Pages: home, about, contact, Far Fetched Labs. A dark redesign was recently implemented (from a claude.ai/design handoff) and is on the `style-edits` branch.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: strategic advisory, platform architecture, team scaling, leadership development, and AI advisory/enablement (AI in the workplace, in engineering, in product).
- Sweet spot is iGaming; extended reach to fintech, media, insurance brokerage, health and fitness, e-commerce.
- Far Fetched Labs is an incubator: concept to prototype and market-ready narrative.
- CTAs: schedule a 30-minute intro call, email info@farfetched.ltd, connect on LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/jessecary).
- Static hosting: no server-side code; interactivity is CSS or small client-side scripts.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Far-Fetched Advisory and Consulting; sub-brand Far Fetched Labs. First-person singular voice appears on About and the home teaser (Jesse), plural "we" elsewhere; reconcile only on request. The existing dark visual identity is incumbent but not yet recorded as binding.

## Evidence on Hand
- One client case study: Betty.ca player segmentation and platform optimization (iGaming, March to July 2025), with logo at `docs/assets/company-logos/clients/`.
- Logo library for clients, direct employers, industry leaders and partners in `docs/assets/company-logos/`.
- Photo of Jesse at the BITR conference: `docs/assets/banner-image-jesse.png`.
- Not on hand: testimonials, quantified results, pricing. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- Credibility before polish: every claim traces to real experience or evidence.
- Make the next step obvious: an intro call is never more than one click away.
- One senior person, not an agency: keep the voice personal and direct.
- Serve three audiences without splitting the site: advisory leads, Labs is a clear secondary path.
